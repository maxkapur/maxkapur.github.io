About

Posted December 1, 2024 · Modified November 7, 2026

Illusion Slopes is a personal website.

Why have a personal website? I’ve revised my goals for this website more times than I can count. Sometimes, the purpose of my website is to cultivate a professional image. Other times, Illusion Slopes is a place to be myself and write about anything other than work. And on yet other days, it’s a tech cauldron—a space where I practice new programming concepts, tighten my version control system, or play at web design, and neglect to write any new posts at all.

In other words, I can’t promise much in terms of consistency or regularity. But generally, when I write, I write about these topics:

  • Numerical optimization and game theory IRL
  • Professional/educational experiences such as teaching English in Korea and the Critical Language Scholarship program
  • Things I read and opinions about them
  • Learning and studying Korean now that I’m no longer in school (Anki shoutout)

Bio

What follows is the biography I copy-paste into LinkedIn. It’s due for an update, but not today.

I currently work as an operations research analyst at a private consulting firm, where I use modeling, simulation, numerical optimization, and algorithmic game theory to solve client problems in a variety of sectors.

In 2022, I completed my master’s degree in industrial engineering at Seoul National University, where my thesis research concerned numerical modeling of college admissions markets. I also worked as a research assistant in the Management Science/Optimization Lab at SNU, where I supported a variety of projects related to linear and nonlinear programming, combinatorial optimization, and urban transit network models.

Prior to my studies at SNU, I taught English for two years as a US State Department Fulbright scholar at a middle school in Naju, South Korea. I graduated from the University of Southern California in 2018 with degrees in jazz piano and Korean in 2018; here’s my senior recital video.

Contact

The best way to reach me is by emailing me at max@maxkapur.com. You can also find me on GitHub, LinkedIn, and YouTube.

I especially love hearing from applicants and current participants about the US State Department’s Fulbright and Critical Language scholarships and the Government of Korea Scholarship, all of which were transformative experiences.
